One of Oman’s most remote beaches

Bar Al Hikman is the kind of place people drive five hours for, and then don’t want to leave.

  • Lagoons you can wade into

    Warm, shallow, clear water that’s safe for a slow swim, a paddle or a kayak at low tide.

  • Flamingos and migrating birds

    The tidal flats draw flamingos and dozens of other species between autumn and spring.

  • Night skies without city light

    No towns nearby and almost no phone signal. Just the campfire, the tide and a sky full of stars.

  • Kite surfing in summer

    Steady monsoon-edge winds make the flat water a known kite spot in the hotter months.

About the place

Longest natural sand bar in the Middle East
Oman’s largest tidal plain, about 2,900 km² of salt flats, inlets and beach.
Around 450 to 500 km from Muscat
Roughly a five-hour drive, with the last stretch across open salt flats.
A true digital detox

Getting there

Part of the magic is the journey. Plan for a full day of driving from Muscat.

  1. Leave Muscat early

    Head south on the coastal and interior roads toward Al Wusta.

  2. Reach Mahout area

    Top up fuel, water and snacks. This is the last easy stop.

  3. Cross the salt flats

    A 4×4 is essential. Follow the tracks the camp shares with you.

  4. Arrive at camp

    Message us on WhatsApp before you lose signal so we can guide you in. Confirm route
